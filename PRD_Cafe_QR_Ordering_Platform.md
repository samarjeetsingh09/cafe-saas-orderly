# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
## Cafe Digital Menu & Table-Side Ordering Platform

**Document Version:** 2.1 (Draft)
**Date:** July 2026
**Status:** Draft — Pending Founder Review

---

## 1. Executive Summary

A subscription-based SaaS platform that lets cafe and restaurant owners replace static/PDF menus with a digital, QR-code-driven menu system that supports **full contactless ordering and payment**, directly from the customer's table — without commissions like aggregator platforms (Zomato/Swiggy) charge.

**One-line pitch:** "Scan, order, pay, eat — no waiter wait, no commission for the owner."

---

## 2. Problem Statement

### For Customers
- Long wait times for a waiter to take an order
- Long wait at reception/counter to pay the bill
- Risk of order mistakes due to verbal communication
- No visibility into order status ("kab tak aayega khana?")

### For Cafe Owners
- Printed menus are expensive and slow to update (new prices, sold-out items, seasonal items)
- Staffing constraints — not enough waiters to serve all tables quickly
- Order errors from manual note-taking increase food waste and customer complaints
- Aggregator platforms (Zomato, Swiggy Dineout) charge high commissions (~20–25%) for similar order/payment conveniences
- No easy access to sales data/analytics to understand what's selling

### Market Context
- QR-code based "static" digital menus are now a commodity — many free tools exist
- The differentiated value is in the **full transactional loop**: order + kitchen routing + payment, not just menu display
- This is a proven model (UrbanPiper, Petpooja, Swiggy Dineout, Popmenu) — the opportunity is a lower-cost, India-first, commission-free, independent alternative for small-to-mid cafes

## 4. Target Users & Personas

### Persona 1: Cafe Owner / Manager ("Rohit, 34, owns a 12-table cafe")
- Wants more table turnover without hiring more staff
- Not very technical — needs a simple, fast dashboard
- Price-sensitive; will compare to free QR-menu tools and aggregator commissions

### Persona 2: Customer ("Anjali, dining with friends")
- Wants to order quickly without waiting for a waiter
- Expects UPI payment to be fast and familiar
- Wants to know order status without asking staff

### Persona 3: Kitchen Staff
- Needs to see incoming orders clearly and in order of priority
- Needs to mark items as ready/served

### Persona 4: Waiter (reduced role, not eliminated)
- Still needed for service, serving food, handling exceptions (e.g., item unavailable, customer assistance)

### Persona 5: Founder / Platform Admin (MVP: single user)
- Needs a bird's-eye view across all onboarded cafes — billing status, subscription renewals, support queries
- Needs fallback access to any cafe's menu for support/troubleshooting, with safeguards against accidental edits
- Needs to control platform access per cafe (enable/disable on subscription status)

## 5. Scope

### 5.1 MVP Scope (Phase 1) — *Finalized direction based on founder discussion*

**In scope:**
1. Cafe owner onboarding & dashboard (web, responsive — works on phone or PC, no separate app needed)
2. **Self-serve digital menu editor for owners** — owner can add/edit/delete items, update prices, toggle sold-out, directly from their own dashboard. Platform team does the *initial* menu setup during onboarding; ongoing changes are owner-managed, with free support available if the owner needs help
3. Per-table QR code generation — generated by the platform team after menu setup and table count is confirmed with owner; owner prints/places QR on physical tables
4. Customer-facing ordering web app (no app download — opens in browser via QR scan)
5. Order placement → **automatic print trigger** sends a Kitchen Order Ticket (KOT) directly to a connected printer in the kitchen (no kitchen screen/KDS in MVP)
6. **Dual payment mode, customer-selected at checkout:**
   - **Online (UPI/Razorpay):** payment captured immediately, order marked "Paid"
   - **Cash:** order placed immediately, payment status "Cash – Pending," collected manually by staff and marked "Collected" afterward
7. Owner/reception dashboard (phone or PC) showing:
   - Total sales today (online + cash combined)
   - Online (Paid) total — amount & order count
   - Cash (Pending Collection) total — amount & order count
   - Per-order / per-table breakdown
   - "Mark as Collected" action for cash orders
   - Support/query submission tool — owner raises an issue, founder/admin resolves it from the admin portal
8. **Founder/Admin Portal** (internal tool, single admin user in MVP) — see Section 6.6 and Section 7 for details
9. Subscription billing for cafe owners — **setup fee + recurring subscription** (see Section 9)

**Deferred to Phase 2+:**
- Order status tracking for customers (Preparing/Ready/Served) — deferred since MVP focus is small cafes where staff bandwidth for status updates is limited
- Dedicated Kitchen Display System (KDS) screen with tap-to-update buttons
- Multi-location chain management dashboard
- Loyalty programs / customer accounts / repeat-order history
- WhatsApp-based customer ordering
- Inventory management / stock-linked availability
- Waiter-assist tablet app
- Table reservation system
- Multi-language menu support
- Role-based access for admin portal (multiple internal users/team) — MVP is single-admin

> **Note to founder:** Menu management was revised from a fully concierge (WhatsApp-managed) model to **owner self-serve with founder oversight + free support**. This reduces your ongoing manual workload while still building platform trust/stickiness through support touchpoints, and gives owners faster control over their own menu.

## 6. Core User Flows

### 6.1 Customer Ordering Flow
1. Customer sits at table, scans table-specific QR code
2. Browser opens menu (no app/login required)
3. Customer browses categories, adds items to cart
4. Before proceeding to payment, a popup requires the customer to enter their **name and phone number** (mandatory — cannot proceed without filling this in)
5. At checkout, customer selects payment mode:
   - **Online:** pays via UPI/Razorpay → order confirmed as "Paid"
   - **Cash:** confirms order without payment → order confirmed as "Cash – Pending"
6. Order confirmation shown to customer
7. Order automatically triggers a printed KOT (Kitchen Order Ticket) at the connected kitchen printer — includes order number, table number, items, and quantities
8. Kitchen prepares food based on printed slip; waiter serves
9. (Cash mode) Staff collects cash at/after serving and marks the order "Collected" on the dashboard

### 6.2 Cafe Owner Onboarding & Setup Flow
1. Prospective owner connects with the platform team (call/WhatsApp) to discuss requirements
2. Platform team gathers requirements: number of tables, menu size/categories, current pain points
3. Setup fee quoted based on number of dishes/categories (minimum ~₹1500)
4. **30% advance payment** collected to confirm the engagement
5. Owner sends initial menu (items, prices, photos) via WhatsApp; platform team builds the *first version* into the system as part of setup
6. Platform team generates table-wise QR codes once menu and table count are finalized
7. Hardware check — confirm if cafe has a printer; recommend a Bluetooth thermal printer if not
8. Test run — a dummy order is placed end-to-end (QR scan → order → auto-print → payment) to confirm everything works before go-live
9. **Remaining 70% payment** collected just before go-live
10. Go-live — platform becomes active for real customer orders; owner now has self-serve access to edit their own menu going forward
11. Recurring subscription billing cycle begins from go-live date

### 6.3 Kitchen Flow
1. Order placed by customer → system automatically sends print command to the kitchen-connected printer (no manual trigger needed)
2. Printed KOT slip appears in kitchen with order number, table number, items, quantities
3. Kitchen prepares food based on the slip; no on-screen status tracking in MVP
4. **Failure handling:** if the printer is offline/unreachable, the system should raise an alert on the reception dashboard so staff can manually relay the order — this prevents orders from silently failing to reach the kitchen

### 6.4 Reception / Owner Dashboard Flow
1. Accessible via phone browser or PC — no separate app, fully responsive
2. Shows real-time: total sales today, online (paid) total, cash (pending) total, per-table/per-order breakdown
3. Staff can mark a cash order "Collected" once payment is received, updating the live reconciliation view

### 6.5 Order Editing, Cancellation & Notifications
**Editing/Cancellation policy (MVP):**
- Free editing allowed only **before** order is placed (while still in cart)
- Once an order is placed (and KOT has printed), **no self-service edit/cancel** in-app — customer requests changes directly with staff, handled manually (consistent with how small cafes already operate)
- Refunds for cancelled online-paid orders are handled manually by staff/owner in MVP — no automated refund flow

**Notifications (Reception/Owner):**
- New order triggers a **popup + sound alert** on the dashboard, e.g., "New Order #1024 — Table 5"
- Popup includes the order number (and ideally table number) so staff immediately know which order to act on
- Kitchen "notification" is effectively the auto-printed KOT slip itself — no separate kitchen alert needed
- Push notifications (for when the dashboard isn't actively open) are deferred to Phase 2, pending a native app/PWA

### 6.6 Founder / Admin Portal Flow
1. Founder logs into a separate internal Admin Portal (single admin user in MVP)
2. **Cafes view:** all onboarded cafes shown as cards (name, key snapshot info); clicking a card opens full details — tables, dishes/categories, owner contact, setup fee status, subscription status
3. **Menu view (per cafe):** read access to each cafe's live, current menu in the same card → detail pattern; founder can also edit directly if needed, but every live edit requires a confirmation prompt ("Are you sure you want to make this change?") before it saves to the database — this exists mainly as a support fallback, since owners manage their own menu day-to-day
4. **QR Codes view:** cafe cards showing that cafe's saved/downloadable table QR codes
5. **Billing & Subscription view:**
   - Automatic reminder message sent to owner 5 days before subscription expiry
   - "Expiring Soon" alert section listing all cafes nearing renewal
   - Manual ON/OFF toggle per cafe — subscription expiry auto-switches the cafe's customer-facing menu OFF; founder manually switches it back ON once payment is received
6. **Support/Query view:** queries submitted by owners from their dashboard appear here as a tracked list (open/resolved) for the founder to act on

### 6.7 Owner Dashboard — Detailed Structure
Design philosophy: **simple, functional, admin-panel style** — not a flashy consumer app. Owners are busy and not necessarily tech-savvy, so clarity beats polish.

**Login:** Phone number + password (set during onboarding)

**Tabs:**
1. **Home** — Today's total sales, split into Online (Paid) and Cash (Pending/Collected); real-time new-order popup + sound alert surfaces here
2. **Orders** — Live feed of all orders (order #, table #, items, amount, payment status); "Mark as Collected" action on cash orders
3. **Menu Management** — Organized **category-wise**:
   - Each category lists its dishes
   - An **"Add Dish"** button inside each category to add a new item to that category
   - An **"Add Category"** button at the top of the page to create a new category
   - Each dish has edit (price/photo/description) and sold-out toggle controls
4. **My QR Codes** — View/download/re-print table QR codes
5. **Billing & Subscription** — Subscription status, expiry date, payment history, "Renew/Pay Now" action
6. **Support / Raise a Query** — Submit a query (visible to founder's admin portal); view status of past queries (Open/Resolved)
7. **Sales Reports** — Basic historical view (e.g., yesterday vs. today, past few days) — lightweight, not deep analytics in MVP

## 8. Non-Functional Requirements

- **Performance:** Menu page should load in <2 seconds on average mobile network conditions in India (3G/4G)
- **Reliability:** Payment success rate >98%; order delivery to kitchen view must not be lost even on brief connectivity drops (queue/retry mechanism)
- **Security:** PCI-DSS-compliant payment handling via gateway (no raw card data stored on platform); secure owner authentication
- **Scalability:** Architecture should support multiple concurrent cafes and tables without performance degradation
- **Usability:** Customer flow must work with zero app download, zero login — pure browser-based QR scan to order
- **Device compatibility:** Must work across common Android/iOS browsers, since most Indian customers will scan with mid-range phones

## 9. Monetization Model

**Finalized structure: One-time Setup Fee + Recurring Subscription**

**1. Setup Fee (one-time, at onboarding)**
- Charged when a cafe owner signs up and the menu is being built
- Scales with **number of dishes/categories** (more items = more data entry/setup effort)
- Minimum charge: **~₹1500** (exact slab structure TBD — e.g., could be minimum + per-item-above-threshold pricing)
- Covers: menu data entry, QR code generation for all tables, initial setup/onboarding effort

**2. Recurring Subscription Fee**
- Charged after setup is complete, on an ongoing basis (frequency — monthly vs. yearly — to be finalized)
- Covers: platform hosting, payment processing infrastructure, owner support, ongoing maintenance

**3. Payment Processing**
- Online (UPI/Razorpay) payments — standard payment gateway transaction fees apply (passed through or absorbed — TBD)
- Cash payments — no gateway fee, but increases reconciliation overhead (handled via dashboard's "Mark as Collected" feature)

> **Note to founder:** Exact ₹ amounts for setup fee slabs and subscription pricing are intentionally left open — these can be finalized closer to pilot launch based on competitor benchmarking (Petpooja, UrbanPiper, local QR-menu tools) and cost-of-service calculations (e.g., payment gateway fees, WhatsApp-based menu management labor cost).

## 11. Risks & Open Questions

- **Support load risk:** Even with self-serve menu management, free support requests from less tech-savvy owners could grow significantly as cafe count increases — worth monitoring once past a handful of cafes.
- **Printer reliability risk:** Automatic print-on-order depends on a stable printer connection (network/Bluetooth) — failure handling (dashboard alert) is necessary but adds complexity.
- **Cash reconciliation risk:** Cash orders rely on staff diligence to mark "Collected" — if skipped, dashboard numbers won't reflect reality. May need a simple end-of-day reminder/prompt.
- **Differentiation risk:** Must clearly communicate "commission-free" advantage vs. aggregators (Zomato/Swiggy) to win owner trust.
- **Open question:** Exact setup fee slab structure (beyond the ₹1500 minimum) and subscription pricing/frequency.
- **Open question:** Payment gateway transaction fee handling — absorbed by platform or passed to cafe/customer?
- **Open question:** Target initial segment — independent single-location cafes confirmed as primary focus; chains deferred to Phase 2.
